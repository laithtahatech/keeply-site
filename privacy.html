Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Keeply handles information when you use the Keeply application and its related services.

Keeply is an independent, non-commercial software development project designed as a personal security vault for organizing passwords, bookmarks, media, documents, and application backups.

Privacy principle: Keeply is designed to minimize information sent outside your device and to use third-party services only when required for features that you explicitly choose to use.

1. Information Stored Locally

Depending on the features you use, Keeply may store information locally on your device, including:

  • Password and account information that you choose to save.
  • Bookmarks and bookmark descriptions.
  • Personal media that you choose to store in the vault.
  • Documents and folders that you choose to store.
  • Application settings and related vault information.
  • Backup metadata required to manage backup operations.

Local vault data is intended to remain on your device unless you explicitly use a feature that transfers data to a third-party service, such as Google Drive backup.

2. Google Account and Google Sign-In

Keeply may use Google Sign-In to allow you to authenticate with your Google Account when you choose to use Google-related features.

When you sign in with Google, Google may provide Keeply with basic account information necessary to identify the selected Google Account and complete authentication, such as your Google account identifier, email address, and basic profile information made available through the authorization process.

Keeply uses this information only to provide authentication and account-related functionality requested by you.

3. Google Drive Data

What Google Drive data may Keeply access?

If you enable Google Drive backup, Keeply requests the Google Drive permission required for its backup feature. The application is designed to use Google's application data storage area (drive.appdata) for Keeply backup data.

How is Google Drive data used?

Google Drive data accessed by Keeply is used only to provide the backup and restore functionality that you explicitly request.

Keeply may create, read, update, or manage its own backup files in the application's Google Drive storage area in order to perform backup and restore operations.

Is Google Drive data sold?

No. Keeply does not sell Google user data.

Is Google Drive data used for advertising?

No. Google user data accessed through Google APIs is not used for targeted advertising, personalized advertising, retargeting, or other advertising purposes.

Is Google Drive data used for AI or machine learning?

No. Keeply does not use Google user data obtained through Google APIs to train or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Is Google Drive data shared with third parties?

Keeply does not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties except where such transfer is necessary to provide a user-facing feature that you have explicitly requested, to protect security, to comply with applicable law, or where otherwise permitted or required by Google's applicable policies.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

Information handled by Keeply is used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and operating the Keeply application.
  • Storing information that you intentionally save in your vault.
  • Authenticating your Google Account when you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Creating and restoring backups that you explicitly request.
  • Maintaining and improving the functionality of the application.
  • Protecting the application and its users against abuse or security issues.

Keeply does not use Google user data for purposes unrelated to the user-facing functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

Keeply does not sell your personal information or Google user data to advertisers, data brokers, or information resellers.

Keeply does not use Google user data to determine credit worthiness, provide lending decisions, or serve advertising.

Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the security and integrity of the application and its users.

6. Data Storage and Security

Keeply is designed with a security-focused architecture. Sensitive vault information is intended to be protected using local security and encryption mechanisms provided by the application.

Backup data stored through Google Drive is handled through the Google Drive service associated with the Google Account that you authorize.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also protect their devices, authentication credentials, and Google Accounts.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored vault data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove it using functionality provided by the application or your device.

Backup data stored in Google Drive may remain there until you delete the backup or use Keeply's available backup management functionality to remove it.

Keeply does not intentionally retain Google Drive backup data longer than necessary to provide the backup functionality you request.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete information stored locally through the application's available deletion functionality or by removing the application and its associated local data from your device, where applicable.

You may also delete Keeply backup data stored in your Google Drive through the application's available backup management functionality or through the Google services associated with your account, where applicable.

If you need assistance regarding deletion of data associated with Keeply, contact the developer using the contact information provided by the application.

9. Google API Services

Keeply's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Google services are operated by Google and may be subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

10. Third-Party Services

Keeply may integrate with third-party services, including Google Sign-In and Google Drive.

These services are operated by their respective providers. Their handling of information may also be governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

11. Children's Privacy

Keeply is intended as a personal security and organization application and is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Keeply's functionality or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Keeply's data practices, please contact the Keeply developer through the developer contact information provided in the application or its Google OAuth configuration.
